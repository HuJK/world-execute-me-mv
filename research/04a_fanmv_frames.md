## 1. cn4M-fH08XY — "[FMV] world.execute(me); - MILI" (땡닢)

### Overall style
- **Medium:** 2D vector motion graphics (After-Effects-style). No people, no drawn characters — the "girl" exists only as system messages and pictograms. The whole MV is the UI of the simulation/OS that the song's narrator *is*.
- **Background:** charcoal grey with a faint vertical brushed-metal / film-stripe texture, heavy vignette; pure black for "boot" moments. Section colour shifts carry the mood: **navy blue** for the gender/role-switch pre-chorus 2, **saturated red** full-screens for errors and EXECUTION, **light grey / white** for "god / administrator" screens and the recovery screen, one **teal** flash for TANGENTS, one pure **BSOD blue** flash at "we can unite".
- **Palette:** monochrome white/grey 95% of the time; accents are rare and meaningful (green check, pink restroom pictograms, red prohibition sign, red EXECUTION).
- **Typography:** thin humanist sans (looks like Segoe UI / Windows-Metro). Lyrics appear *as UI copy* — labels on progress bars, dialog text, console lines — mostly sentence case, sometimes letter-spaced caps ("S W I T C H  M Y  G E N D E R", "> E X E C U T I O N"). CAPS keywords are almost never shown as the word itself; they are replaced by a **system status message** ("Protection process is completed.", "Objects (me, you) are created.", "Initializing is completed."). Several intentional/unintentional misspellings: "DIMENTION", "ANTIOXIDENTS", "Clam = 0".
- **Recurring motifs:** glowing power-button icon (opens and closes the film), wireframe isometric **cube = "the simulation"**, progress/loading bars, **door icons** with a red ⃠ prohibition sign (abandonment), the "**Feeling Index**" variable table, RGB chromatic-aberration glitch bands as hard transitions, heart built from triangles.
- **Credits as code:** title card types `> Mili.produce(song);` / `> Eodslv.produce(video[6]);`.

## 2. sNZUo2Oy3KQ — "【Lobotomy Corporation】world.execute(me);" (mochi mikan / もちみかん)

### Overall style
- **Medium:** flat 2D digital fan-art (simple cel shading, thin dark outlines, some hand-drawn marker doodles) animated as mostly-static illustrations, with kinetic text and hard cuts. Low budget, high concept.
- **Frame:** letterboxed; **English lyric subtitle in a black top bar, Japanese translation in a black bottom bar** (both white sans, Segoe-like). A second English layer is often typed in big black serif-ish sans *inside* the picture word by word ("If" → "If I'm" → "If I'm a set of points").
- **Setting:** the **Lobotomy Corporation facility map** — a central 4×3 grid of rooms (main department) surrounded by tilted outlying room tiles. It appears dark grey (default/"off"), pale mint on white (when Angela is "on"), and with CRT scanline noise (corrupted / chorus 2b).
- **Characters:**
  - **Angela** (the game's AI secretary) — long pale-cyan hair in a high side ponytail with a red tie, black suit, white shirt, red necktie. Eyes **closed** in chorus 1 and chorus 3; **open, amber/orange** at the first EXECUTION and through the whole love section. She is shown as a black silhouette elsewhere.
  - **X / the manager** — a short-haired black silhouette, gets a white heart on his chest and later an angel halo.
  - Employee/agent OCs for the food verse (purple-haired masked man, orange-haired girl with red headband and "N" armband, brown-haired cat-girl with tabby ears and tail), a green-haired grinning character holding a pill and syringe *(possibly the Sephirah Netzach — uncertain)*.
  - The **brain-in-a-ring emblem** (cyan brain/tree inside a red circle; later glowing lime green) — appears to be the Lobotomy Corp logo *(identification fairly confident)*.
- **Palette:** black / white / grey with mint-cyan (Angela, map), red (tie, keywords, love), lime green (corrupted emblem), amber eyes.
- **CAPS keyword treatment:** usually the word itself, big, set into the illustration ("SIMULATIONS", "SATISFACTION", "EXECUTION" in black; "CIRCUMFERENCE", "LO-O-OVE" in red), or a hand-written doodle word inside a thought bubble ("NUTRIENTS", "ANTIOXIDANTS", "ENJOYMENT"). In-game systems substitute for some ("Vertically Synchronize Terminal", "Assign All Employees", "Weakening Deterrence of Qliphoth", "1st–7th Trumpet").

- **Medium:** hand-drawn 2D animation, Japanese indie/art-anime sensibility (closing credits: "Director / Animation / Editor **sowiti**", "Composite Support Shun Yamaguchi", "Special Thanks Seiji Nomura, Naoki Yoshibe", "Music Mili"). ## 4. Cross-video synthesis

### (a) The 10 most striking or clever lyric-visual ideas

1. **"If I approach infinity… LIMITATIONS" → `lim(n→∞) X/n = A`** (sNZUo2Oy3KQ). A real limit expression where X is the player and the answer resolves into the AI heroine's silhouette marked "A". It visualises the lyric and the relationship at the same time.
2. **The bridge as a live-typed chat log** (sNZUo2Oy3KQ): "Angela>>Do you believe that sometimes things are more beautiful when they are forgotten? / You told me so someday. / I believe it now." Eleven seconds of black and typing make the most emotional beat in the set. It is the closest existing precedent for an AI-in-a-chat-window MV, so we must do something different with ours.
3. **Escalation inside repetition** (sNZUo2Oy3KQ). The EXECUTION section uses one static frame, but the Japanese subtitle shifts from 実行します ("will execute") to 抹殺します ("will annihilate"). The count-in becomes "1st Trumpet … 7th Trumpet", one colour per number. The repeated hook stays still while the meaning escalates.
4. **Pronoun flip in red**: "If ***you're*** the only god / Then ***I'm*** the proof of ***your*** existence" (sNZUo2Oy3KQ). Two coloured words reverse who is god and who is creation. A halo appears on the human and a wing on the AI.
5. **Lyric as system message** (cn4M-fH08XY): "⚠ Variable 'me.cansee' is disabled." for *blind my vision*; "Objects (me, you) are created." for OBJECT CREATION; "GOD IS ALWAYS TRUE" for *you have made some*; "[Access Denied]" for *challenging your god*. These carry the wit through copywriting instead of imagery.
6. **The "Feeling Index" and its reprise** (cn4M-fH08XY). In chorus 1, the object "You" has Happy = 0 while Confused and Empty = 1. In chorus 2b, the object "Me" shows a red column of `[Undefined] = 1 … lifeless = 1;`, followed by "[Reference Error] It is just Undefined Error." on a red screen. Emotions are shown as variables, and the reprise inverts whose feelings are measured.
7. **Time travel as a timeline scrubber** (cn4M-fH08XY): the marker slides 1828 → 2017 NOW → AD, then "<Press any button to reunite>". Also **SYSTEM RECOVERY "(Y/N) Y Confirmed."** for *if I can have you back*. Both are UI metaphors that read instantly.
8. **Verse 1 as embryology** (g0g6QDaSRrc). An ink drop becomes points, then a ring of dots, an egg, a two-cell embryo (CIRCUMFERENCE), a sine-wave strand, a DNA helix (TANGENTS) and growing tissue (infinity). The geometry lyrics turn into the biology of becoming alive.
9. **Abandonment shown by absence** (sNZUo2Oy3KQ and cn4M-fH08XY). In sNZUo2Oy3KQ the beloved's silhouette fades to white outline-first and then the whole world bleaches away for *you have left*, and Angela vanishes from the frame on every EXECUTION in chorus 3. In cn4M-fH08XY a row of doors is crossed out one by one, and a running-man exit sign leaves through a door for *though you are free*.
10. **Loop and evolution before "we are trapped"** (g0g6QDaSRrc). *If I can have you back… EXECUTION* plays the March of Progress, then the intro shots replay exactly (phone, crosswalk, apple). The film says "trapped" through structure, not words. A runner-up from the same film: the glyph alphabet takes over the lyrics as the song progresses, and every toggle on a wall flips to the same word, "disheartened".

Honourable mentions: the AC = sine wave / DC = flat line split-screen swipe and the sun→moon swipe for AM→PM (sNZUo2Oy3KQ); "Pinocchio" typed out for ILLEGAL ARGUMENTS (sNZUo2Oy3KQ); TANGENTS as a balance-scale beam resting tangent to a red wave (sNZUo2Oy3KQ); the giant counter "9% → 18% → 61% MEMORY" for approaching infinity (cn4M-fH08XY); the lyric typed inside a cat's iris for *only god* (g0g6QDaSRrc); the count-in shown as six dots filling under "ANNOUNCE" (cn4M-fH08XY).

### (b) Clichés to avoid (seen here, or standard for this song)

- **Power-button bookends** and the "boot → shutdown" frame. cn4M-fH08XY owns this, and so does nearly every world.execute FMV.
- **A wireframe cube or "the Matrix" as THE SIMULATION**, plus terminal green-on-black logs ("> succeed in executing world").
- **Progress or loading bars as the answer to every CAPS word.** Use them once at most.
- **RGB chromatic-aberration bars and datamosh glitch as all-purpose transitions.** cn4M-fH08XY uses them more than ten times; they read as filler.
- **Full-frame red "ERROR" screens** for DISHEARTENED or EXECUTION, and **giant red "EXECUTION" letters slamming in**.
- **"AI turns evil" eye-change at EXECUTION** (Angela's eyes snap open amber). For a friendly-assistant protagonist, avoid HAL red eyes, menacing smiles and annihilation subtext.
- **Angel halo and wings for god or EXISTENCE.** Both cn4M-fH08XY and sNZUo2Oy3KQ did it.
- **♂/♀ symbols, restroom pictograms or riding crops and ropes for gender and S/M.** They are literal, and the S/M imagery is risqué for an AI-brand piece.
- **Cut to black for "blind my vision".** sNZUo2Oy3KQ did it, and it is the first idea anyone has.
- **Clip-art eggplant, tomato and cat icons in a box.** Three of three videos showed the literal foods.
- **Building every line word by word in giant type over a static character** (sNZUo2Oy3KQ chorus 1 and 3). It is cheap and gets tiring after one chorus.
- **Low-poly or wireframe hearts for LOVE.**
- **Noose, gun-to-a-child and shackle imagery** (g0g6QDaSRrc). It is powerful in an art film but inappropriate for a cute AI-mascot MV, and the noose implies self-harm.

- **Typos in the keywords** ("DIMENTION", "ANTIOXIDENTS", "SIMULATIONS" for STIMULATIONS). Proofread every on-screen word.
